# Box clip council — strategist ruling (investor audience and positioning)

Client: hippo. Date: 2026-11-21. Piece: 15–18 s vertical one-take "phone footage" clip. Internal working note.
**ASSUMPTION (unchanged since 2026-11-18):** audience = Saudi franchise investors (individuals, family businesses, F&B operators). No approved strategy or research exists; this is judgement, not tested with investors.

## 1. The idea: «ما ينزل خفيف» — runner-up «محل للإيجار»
- **Pick: «ما ينزل خفيف».** It is the only one of the three built on HIPPO's own approved idea (film scene 5: «هيبو إذا نزل، ما ينزل خفيف»), so the clip and the 40 s film sell one thought. It stops a feed in the first second, and the regions where HIPPO wants partners have no HIPPO customers yet, so the clip has to work on people who have never seen the brand.
- **Runner-up: «محل للإيجار».** The most literal investor picture (empty shop becomes a HIPPO), but quiet in a feed, and the key says "the shop is handed to you", which a paid franchise is not. If it is ever made: no key, card only.
- **Rejected: «الطلب الغلط».** It talks to existing diners in Riyadh and Madinah, the two regions HIPPO is not recruiting in, and «هذا فرعك» reads as a gift.
- **Is a fake-candid spectacle credible to investors?** Yes, as the top of the funnel only. The clip earns the scan; the card, the form and the brochure carry the seriousness. The stunt may be absurd; the card may not. From the moment the box opens: no joke, steady frame, real logo, real facts.

## 2. The card (about 4 seconds of reading), top to bottom
1. HIPPO logo (the real file) with the label «امتياز هيبو التجاري». The word «امتياز» must be readable, or the clip is only a food ad.
2. Headline: **«افتح هيبو في مدينتك.»**
3. One proof line: **«علامة سعودية منذ 2021 · 5 فروع في منطقتين»** (Western numerals; «منطقتين», never «مدينتين»; no Jeddah).
4. **One QR code: the franchise form**, with «امسح الرمز وتواصل معنا.»
- **No franchise terms in the clip or its caption.** A fee of 50,000 on its own reads as the whole cost, while the total investment is deliberately left to be agreed per site; and 5 % / 2 % / 5 years cannot be read or weighed in 4 seconds. Terms stay in the brochure and the conversation after the form. From my general knowledge, not verified here: franchise offers in Saudi Arabia are regulated (disclosure and registration duties), which is a second reason to keep terms out of a social clip. This is not legal advice; the client's adviser confirms.
- **Menu QR: not in this clip.** Two codes halve the scan rate of the one that matters, and the clip has a single job. This departs from the client's «خليها الاثنين» for the film and brochure; tell him.
- **A QR inside a video cannot be scanned by the phone that is playing it.** The form link must also sit in the caption or profile link, and the card must be held flat, sharp and still for at least 3 s.

## 3. Closing line
- **«افتح هيبو في مدينتك»** is the last thing read: it is the offer and the action, and it is the client's own preference for investor material.
- **«ما ينزل خفيف»** is the hook: caption first line and, if any voice is used, the only spoken line.
- **«حُطّ ثقلك» stays out of this clip.** Here the weight that lands is HIPPO's, not the investor's; two weight lines in 15 s compete; and the client has already removed it from investor print. It remains the 40 s film's line.

## 4. Honesty and Saudi norms
- **Disclose in the caption:** «إعلان · مشهد تمثيلي». If the clip is AI-generated, also switch on the platform's AI-content label (ASSUMPTION: the main platforms require it for realistic generated footage; check each before posting).
- **Post from HIPPO's own account.** Never seed it on anonymous or "found footage" accounts as a real event. Any paid creator repost carries «إعلان».
- **Make the staging plain on screen:** a sandwich box that rocks cars is visibly impossible; keep it that exaggerated, and let the brand reveal come within the take, not after it.
- **Scene fixes:** no person, child or animal anywhere near the landing bay; the box falls from open sky, never from a balcony, window or a person's hand (throwing objects from height) and never from a drone (implies a delivery service); cars rock and chirp but show no dent or broken glass; the agency's own or hired cars, plates unreadable, no car badges in close-up; no identifiable bystanders, women and families above all; no mosque, government building or recognisable mall/property in frame; alarms short and not at night in a residential street.
- **Carried over from my 2026-11-18 ruling:** no city name, map, storefront or queue. The car park is anonymous, so «نزل» stays a box landing, not a claimed market launch.
- The reaction voice (any exclamation) is the dialect expert's call; I flag only that a religious phrase must not be played as the punchline.

## 5. Risks, ranked
1. **It entertains diners and converts no investor.** Mitigation: «امتياز» on the card and in the caption's first line; link in caption/profile; card held 3 s; the caption names the regions sought, worded exactly as in brochure v08 («نبحث عن شركاء في…»); judge the clip by form submissions, not views.
2. **Fake brand assets, if AI-generated.** The outside montage reviewed today invented five logos. Mitigation: the box art, logo, card text and QR are composited from the real files, never generated; the QR is decoded from the final export; the Arabic is proofread on the frame.
3. **Read as deceptive, or as an unlawful franchise advertisement.** Mitigation: section 4 disclosure; no terms, no returns, no figures beyond the confirmed proof line; the client's legal adviser confirms the offer may be advertised (not legal advice). Before traffic is sent: the form page's privacy links were empty on 2026-11-18 — recheck.

**Open assumptions:** audience definition; platform AI-label rules; Saudi franchise and advertising rules (general knowledge, unverified); that the client accepts one QR here. I have not seen the reference clip or any footage.
