# HIPPO — box clip «ما ينزل خفيف»: council ruling, expert 1 of 3 (verbal identity, Arabic copy, VO)

Date: 2026-11-21. Piece: 17 s vertical one-take phone-style clip. Sources: `brand-context.md`, script v03, my tone ruling of 2026-11-18.
Limits: I am not a native human proofreader or a Saudi voice talent. Line lengths are syllable estimates, not timed audio; nothing was heard. A Saudi reader must say every line aloud before it is recorded or generated.

## 1. Voice — ruling: (c) BOTH, strictly separated
The filmer reacts during the take; Fahad speaks only once the card is in frame. The reaction sells the "phone footage"; Fahad's clean voice arriving over the card is the moment the clip admits it is an ad, which is also the honest thing to do.
- The filmer must NOT be Fahad's voice. Best: a real Saudi man recorded on a phone, mumbled and slightly off-mic. If it is generated and sounds acted, cut it and keep breath and footsteps only (fallback = option b). A fake reaction is worse than none.
- Avoided on purpose: «بسم الله» and «يا ساتر» (religious phrases inside an ad), «والله» (an oath), anything rude, and any full sentence.
- Reaction options (pick by ear): «أوف!… وش ذا؟!» (recommended) / «لا لا لا… وش ذا؟» / «هاه؟! وش اللي طاح؟» / «يا رجّال!»

- Proof: all three confirmed facts stay, as two units, not three. «5 فروع» is the strongest (a working system), «منذ 2021» says it has lasted, «سعودية» costs one word. The two regions are left to the brochure. No other figure; no fee, royalty or return on this card.
- ONE QR code: the franchise form (the client's own code). The menu code is left off here: two codes side by side in a 4-second shot make the phone lock on the wrong one. This differs from the film's two-code end card and is a ruling for this clip only.
- Western numerals; Tajawal; no letter-spacing; the English line does not typeset the word HIPPO outside the logo.

## 3. On-screen super — ruling: NONE
A designed super in the first nine seconds kills the phone-footage illusion, and after that the card is the typography. The hook text belongs to the platform's own caption layer (section 4), in the app's native font.

## 4. Social caption
«هيبو إذا نزل، ما ينزل خفيف. امتياز هيبو التجاري: قدّم طلبك من الرابط.»
«مشهد تمثيلي.»
- The staging note is needed: the clip imitates real footage of a falling object and rocking cars. If it is AI-generated, also switch on the platform's AI-content label.
- Most viewers watch on the phone they would scan with, so the form link must also be in the caption, bio or link sticker.
- Hook captions (first second, none claims the event was real): 1. «وش اللي طاح في المواقف؟» 2. «خفيف؟ اسمع الصوت.» 3. «انتظر لين ينفتح البوكس.»

## 5. Closing line — ruling
SPOKEN: «حُطّ ثقلك مع هيبو.» It pays off the thud, and it is an approved Fahad line. WRITTEN: «افتح هيبو في مدينتك.» On a card read in four seconds the plain instruction wins, and it matches the brochure.

## Conditions for the director
- The car park stays unidentifiable: no mall name, signage, landmarks or readable plates. «ما ينزل خفيف» must stay tied to the box, not to a real place or market (script v03 rule).
- The card is held flat and still, with the QR fully in frame from about 12.5 s to the end.
