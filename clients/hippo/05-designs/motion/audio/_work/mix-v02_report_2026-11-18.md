## 3. Not achieved, changed, or worked around
- **Hook absolute level.** The direction asked for about -7 dB RMS. Under a -1.5 dBTP ceiling without v01's square-wave clipping the hook reaches -10.7. The order and the 2 dB lead are met by setting the box and CTA hits lower.
- **`sfx_impact_mid.mp3` is not a sustained mid-range impact.** Above 250 Hz it is -20 dBFS for the first 100 ms and -35 after, with a 19 dB crest. On its own it left the phone-band hook 4.9 LU short. The 2.3 LU result comes from adding the hook file's own rumble, high-passed at 250 Hz and raised about 16 dB, plus a pitched-down paper hit. This is EQ on existing material and has not been heard; it could sound rough.
- **`sfx_whoosh.mp3` peaks at 0.15 s and then dies away**, the opposite of "rises right up to 0.92". It is used reversed, trimmed to its rise and slowed 27 % to fill 0.45–0.92. Whether it still reads as a fall is unverified.
- **`sfx_squish.mp3` is very quiet** (peak -23.6 dBFS). Usable with gain; it sits at -18.9 dBFS.
- **`sfx_paper.mp3`**: seven usable hits found (0.68, 1.73, 2.09, 3.44, 5.64, 6.37, 6.68 s) and reused at different pitches for 15 placements. The "lid creak" is three pitched-down taps, not a creak.
- **Music cannot follow the direction's curve.** It measures 120 BPM (not 92) and holds -15 dBFS ±1 dB every second, with no build or thinning. It is slowed 2.44 % (pitch about 0.4 semitone down) so it enters on a beat at 3.000 and turns its phrase on 19.400. The scene-3 landings (1.3 s apart) and the scene-5 roll are not on its grid.
- **Music tail and logo.** "Runs to the end" and "the logo is the last sound in clear air" pull against each other. The music fades from 37.3 and is below the room tone by about 38.6.
- **«أثقل».** The word is a loud first syllable and a quiet rest. +3 dB moved it 0.3 dB because the voice peak limiter holds the first syllable, so +5 dB is applied: the whole word gains 1.1 dB over v01 and its second half 2.7 dB. «ثقيل» gains 2.6 dB. It still has the second-lowest margin over music (5.7 dB).
- **Voice peak limiter added** (-3 dBFS in the voice stem) so the master limiter is left for the hits. It costs the other lines 0.6–1.4 dB RMS against v01 (line 2 the most).
- **Master limiter works about 4.4 dB on the hook's first transient.** Nothing else plays there, so nothing else is pulled down.
- **Box boom is used from its left channel only.** The stereo file is out of phase on the attack (correlation -0.55) and loses 6 dB when summed to mono, as on a phone.
- **Room tone, reverb and sonic logo are synthesised** (filtered noise; a 0.3 s noise-decay room on effects only; sine tones). The voice is dry.
- **ffmpeg quirk.** In ffmpeg 6.1 `asoftclip=oversample=4` halves the level. The script now oversamples explicitly with a fixed 0.513 gain, so it does not depend on that behaviour.
