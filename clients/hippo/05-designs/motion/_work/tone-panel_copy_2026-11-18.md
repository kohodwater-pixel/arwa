# HIPPO — tone panel, expert 2 of 2: verbal identity and Arabic copy

Date: 2026-11-18. Reviewed: direction «أ» scene table in `hippo_franchise-motion-ad_concept-script_ar_v02.md`. Script not edited.
Limit of this review: I am not a native human proofreader or a Saudi voice talent. These are reasoned rulings; a native Saudi reader must read the final lines aloud before recording.

## 1. Tone overall: KEEP WITH CHANGES
The playfulness is dry confidence, not clowning: short sentences, one idea (weight), and a deliberate turn to a calmer register in scenes 7–9. That suits an investor as long as the joke is always about the brand's weight and never about the listener. «حُطّ ثقلك» is used correctly: in Saudi usage it means commit fully / put your weight behind something, and it is an invitation, not an order. «ثقيل» is heard as praise when it is attached to a person's standing or a name; it turns negative in exactly two settings — a guest (ضيف ثقيل، ثقيل دم) and food (أكل ثقيل على المعدة). The script walks into the first in scene 6 and brushes the second throughout, so the weight must stay tied to «حضور», the box and the brand, never to the eating. Dialect is consistently white Saudi (إيه، بس، مليانة، تشوف، تشيله); no Egyptian or Levantine drift. The MSA of «امتياز هيبو التجاري» in scene 9 is a correct, useful shift to a serious register.

| 3 | Screen: item names / VO «بس الحضور…» | KEEP the words. ADJUST placement: say it on the third landing (about 0:10), not at 0:07, or the sentence hangs for five seconds before «ثقيل». Names follow the kit («الحلومي» with ال is the kit's spelling). |
| 4 | Screen «خبز خفيف. حضور ثقيل.» / VO «…ثقيل.» | KEEP. «حضور ثقيل» is slightly unusual («حضور قوي» is the everyday phrase) but the contrast with «خفيف» makes it read as wordplay and praise. Do not swap in «وزن ثقيل»: on food it reads as weight gain. |

## 3. The three changes I insist on
1. Scene 6: «وبوكس الجَمعات؟ أثقل.» on screen and in VO (removes the "heavy guest" reading, names the product as the kit names it).
2. Scene 7: screen «ناقصها ثقلك.» without the colon; VO without «مثل ما تشوف».
3. Scene 9: VO «امتياز هيبو التجاري… والكفّة تنتظرك.»

None of the replacements adds a figure, a claim or a named market. Not insisted, but do it: move «بس الحضور…» to the third landing in scene 3.
