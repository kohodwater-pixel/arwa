# Tone panel — strategist verdict (investor audience and positioning)

Client: hippo. Date: 2026-11-18. Reviewed: `hippo_franchise-motion-ad_concept-script_ar_v02.md`, direction «أ» «حُطّ ثقلك». Internal working note; the script was not edited.
**ASSUMPTION:** audience = Saudi/Gulf franchise investors (individuals, family businesses, F&B operators). No approved strategy or research exists; this is judgement, not tested with investors.

## 1. Verdict: KEEP WITH CHANGES
The playful tone is right for scenes 1–6 and wrong as the last word. A franchise investor is buying a consumer brand's pull, so a film that shows the brand's character at full strength is the evidence, and with no figures allowed it is the only evidence available. A sober corporate film would have nothing to say. The film's job is to earn an enquiry, not to close. The weakness is the hand-over: from scene 7 the brand stops talking to diners and starts asking for someone's capital, yet the voice keeps winking through to the final line, the word «امتياز» first appears at 0:33, and no line tells the investor what to do next. The wit should stay; the film needs a change of register at the turn and a plain ending.

No line promises returns, figures or named markets. Implied-claim risk sits in scenes 4, 5 and 9 as noted. Seriousness at the CTA is **not sufficient as written**; the three changes below fix it.

## 3. Changes I insist on (maximum three)
1. **Plain ending (scene 9).** Use the VO and on-screen replacements above. The last spoken line is the one place the joke stops.
2. **Name the offer at the turn (scene 7).** Add the on-screen label «امتياز هيبو التجاري» at 0:24, not only on the end card.
3. **Two registers, written into the voice direction.** Scenes 1–6 keep the wit. Scenes 7–9 use the same voice, steady and warm, with no comedy timing. Production rule alongside it: no imagery of branches, cities, queues or maps, and the single circle is never captioned or animated as a limited or last place.

## Assumptions and risks
- The audience definition is an assumption (see top).
- The client has not confirmed that the franchise is open for enquiries, or in which markets. The film cannot be released with an empty contact slot.
- From my general knowledge, not verified here: franchise offers in Saudi Arabia are regulated (disclosure and registration duties). The client's legal adviser should confirm the offer may be advertised. This note is not legal advice.
- «ثقيل» carries negative senses in some uses (an unwelcome guest, heavy on the stomach). That is for the dialect expert to rule on; I flag it only.
