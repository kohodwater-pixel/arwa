## Method notes

- PDF: words extracted with PyMuPDF and grouped into visual lines; a price is a number followed by `RUB` or `SAR`. Each hotel's pages must yield exactly its priced extras then its rate rows, in table order. Analysis pages may only repeat pairs that exist in rates.json.
- DOCX: python-docx reads every table whose header contains «السعر بالريال»; rate tables (one per period), extras tables and analysis tables are checked separately.
- Arabic copy/search in the PDF: Chromium writes some Arabic ligatures (for example «لا» and «بر») in visual order in the text layer, so searching or copying a word with these ligatures can fail. The printed shapes are correct; numbers are unaffected.
