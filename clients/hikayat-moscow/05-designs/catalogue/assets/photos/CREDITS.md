## Verification limits (stated honestly)

- Licence and title come from the Openverse API record (queried 2026-11-15). The Flickr landing pages could not be opened from the build environment (proxy returned 403), so the licence was **not** re-checked on Flickr itself. Re-check before print release.
- CC BY-SA 2.0 (National photo): the photo is used unmodified apart from scaling and cropping in layout; the share-alike condition applies to the photo, not to the catalogue as a whole (collection use). Confirm with the client whether this is acceptable.
- The National photo is 683 x 1024 px; it is placed small (about 60 mm wide), which gives roughly 290 ppi effective. The city photos are 1024 px wide and are placed at a size that keeps them above about 190 ppi; acceptable for screen and office print, but **below 300 ppi for commercial print**.
