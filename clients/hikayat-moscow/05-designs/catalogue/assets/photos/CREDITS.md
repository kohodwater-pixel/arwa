## Verification limits

- Licence and title come from the Openverse API record (queried 2026-11-15). The Flickr landing pages could not be opened from the build environment (the proxy returned 403), so the CC0 mark was **not** re-checked on Flickr itself. Re-check before print release.
- Both city photos are about 1024 px wide. As placed (174 mm wide), they give about 150 ppi: fine for screen, WhatsApp, email and office printing, but **below the 300 ppi needed for commercial print**.
