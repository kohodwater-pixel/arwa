# Name and descriptor decision — otlobny-emaar

**Date:** 2026-11-14  **Prepared by:** naming-specialist, for the Creative Director
**Status:** AGENCY DECISION PENDING CLIENT CONFIRMATION. The client delegated the decision while he is unavailable. Every string below is the agency's ruling. It is not final until the client confirms it against his Commercial Registration (CR 4650245200).

**Not legal advice.** This is a naming and orthography decision. It is not a trademark clearance and not a legal check of the registered name. Before the name is used on signage, contracts or a trademark filing, a formal trademark search in Saudi Arabia (SAIP) and a check against the CR certificate are required.

## 2. Decision table

| # | Point | Decision | Reason | Confidence | Client must still confirm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arabic: «إعمار» or «اعمار» | **«إعمار» with hamza** in the descriptor and in all running text. The wordmark stays «اطلبني» with no hamza, by client ruling. | «إعمار» is the verbal noun of أَعْمَرَ (form IV). Its alif carries a hamza under it, and leaving it out is an informal typing habit, not an alternative spelling. A formal legal line under a logo has to be orthographically correct. The profile has used «إعمار» since v01, so the hamza form changes nothing in the current text. The one place where the client's own spelling is kept on purpose is the wordmark «اطلبني», as he ruled. Using no hamza there and a hamza in «إعمار» is consistent: the logo is a brand mark and the descriptor is typeset text. | Medium-high | Whether the CR certificate writes «إعمار» or «اعمار». The Ministry of Commerce systems often drop the hamza. If the CR shows «اعمار», the descriptor still uses «إعمار», because the hamza is spelling and not a different name, unless the client asks for a character-for-character copy of the CR. |
| 2 | Arabic legal form | **«شركة اطلبني إعمار المحدودة»**, the client's form with the two spelling errors corrected (اطليني → اطلبني, اعمار → إعمار) | This is the form the client supplied. «شركة … المحدودة» is the established short Arabic form for a limited liability company in Saudi Arabia, and it matches the original profile's statement that the company is «شركة ذات مسؤولية محدودة». The full form «شركة اطلبني إعمار ذات المسؤولية المحدودة» is too long to sit under the wordmark at the width it has in the client's image, and it adds no information to a profile. The full legal wording stays where it already is, on the p5 fact line «الشكل القانوني: شركة ذات مسؤولية محدودة». | Medium | That the company is still an LLC under the new name (not a single-person company or another form), and that «اطلبني إعمار» is the trade name now on the CR. The old CR name was «اطلبني للصيانة والنظافة والتشغيل», and the rename may not be registered yet. |
| 3 | English: EMAR / Emaar / E'mar / Imar | **EMAR** (title case "Emar" in running text) | (a) The client supplied it himself in the one Latin line he clearly checked. (b) "Emaar" is the exact Latin spelling of Emaar Properties, a famous mark active in Saudi Arabia, including in real estate and construction-related services. "OTLOBNY Emaar" for a contracting and maintenance company invites association and, at worst, a confusion objection. "EMAR" does not remove the phonetic overlap, but it stops the name from reproducing that brand's written mark. This is a risk judgement, not legal advice. (c) The scholarly romanisations "Iʿmār", "I'mar" and "Imar" are correct but read wrongly to English speakers: the apostrophe gets dropped or garbled, and "Imar" is pronounced "eye-mar". "E'mar" adds an apostrophe that breaks in URLs, handles and file names. (d) EMAR is four letters and spells the way most Saudis say it. (e) Domains: both otlobny.co and otlobny.net carry only OTLOBNY, so no spelling is constrained by the web addresses, and OTLOBNY stays the lead element. | Medium | That EMAR is his intended Latin spelling. If the CR has an official English name (many do not), the descriptor follows the CR. |
| 4 | English legal-form line | **OTLOBNY EMAR COMPANY LTD.**, kept exactly as the client wrote it | It mirrors the Arabic line word for word: شركة = Company, المحدودة = Ltd. "Company Ltd." is accepted English usage for a Saudi LLC. "Co. Ltd." is a stylistic abbreviation with no advantage, and "LLC" would depart from what the client typed. All caps suits a short Latin descriptor. Set it in tracked capitals in the Latin font only; the Arabic line is never tracked (`arabic-design.md`). | Medium-high | Nothing beyond point 3, unless the CR has a registered English name. |
| 5 | Running text in the profile | **Change it.** Every English "OTLOBNY Emaar" becomes **"OTLOBNY Emar"**, across the whole profile, the lockup proposal in `04-identity/` and the mockup composites that carry the descriptor. Arabic running text stays **«اطلبني إعمار»**, which is unchanged. The legal form appears only in the descriptor line and the p5 legal-form fact, not in every mention. | One name, one spelling everywhere. Leaving "Emaar" in the body text under an "EMAR" logo line would be an inconsistency, which fails `quality-standards.md`. Under `branding.md` ("rules made late apply everywhere"), every existing deliverable is checked against the change. v08 has 20 occurrences of "Emaar" to change in v09. The repo slug `otlobny-emaar` and existing file names stay as they are: they are internal identifiers that the client never sees. | High, given point 3 | Comes with point 3: if the client rejects EMAR, revert to Emaar in one pass. |

Setting notes for the designer, not part of the strings:
- Use «إعمار» with the hamza-below character U+0625 (إ), not a plain alif with a combining mark.
- In the Arabic line, keep the full stop off the end. In the English line the full stop belongs to "LTD." and stays.
- Arabic is never letter-spaced. If the two lines need to match in width, scale the Arabic line up, as `arabic-design.md` requires, rather than tracking it.
