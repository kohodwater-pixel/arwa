## Conventions applied throughout

- **Name.** Always «اطلبني إعمار» / "OTLOBNY Emaar" in running text. ASSUMPTION: the name, its Latin spelling and the legal form («شركة اطلبني إعمار» / "OTLOBNY Emaar Co.") are not yet confirmed by the client.
- **Spelling of the name.** The old profile writes both «أطلبني» (pp6, 7, 15) and «اطلبني» (pp1, 11, 12, 20). This outline uses «اطلبني» everywhere, matching the logo wordmark and the cover.
- **Old-profile errors corrected.** "Mantinance" (pp1, 20), "WEBSIUT" (p1), «الى» → «إلى» (pp6, 7), «أنتظار» → «انتظار» (p16), «وفقا لمعاير» → «وفقاً لمعايير» (p14), «للعقد» → «لعقد» (p15, sentence not reused anyway), «الانشاء» / «انظمة» → «الإنشاء» / «أنظمة» (p11), «إستراتيجية» → «استراتيجية» (p8), «مقاهي» → «المقاهي» (p14), Latin comma «,» → Arabic «،» (p5), "Abdulmajyed" → "Abdulmajeed" (pp1, 20).
- **Numerals.** Western (0123) in both languages. The «م» after years is dropped («2021», «2030»).
- **Not reused.** The claim «الشركة المختارة لعقد النظافة والصيانة في السوق السعودي لعام 2021م» (p15) and «تعتبر من الشركات الرائدة» (pp13, 14). The Vision 2030 logo (p9) is not reproduced; the reference stays in the vision text only.
- **Nothing invented.** No figures, certificates, clients, projects, team names or logistics details beyond what the brief allows.

## Recommended format (for the art director and the Creative Director to confirm)

- A4 landscape, **20 pages** (a multiple of 4, suits saddle-stitch printing, same length as the old profile). The content does not honestly fill 24 pages; pages are not padded.
- **One bilingual structure on every page:** Arabic block on the right (primary, set larger), English block on the left, imagery shared. No line-by-line interleaving. Page numbers and section labels follow the Arabic reading order (the document opens from the right).
- Old-profile photographs are embedded at about 1240 × 875 px: at 300 dpi that is roughly 105 × 74 mm. They cannot run full-page in print. Image needs below say where a new or higher-resolution image is required.
