# Partner logos — «شركاء النجاح» (27 = 18 existing + 9 new)

Prepared 2026-11-13 by the corporate-designer with `06-profiles/_work/prepare_partner_logos.py`. Contact sheet: `06-profiles/_work/partners_contact-sheet.png`.

Rules followed: nothing redrawn, recoloured, sharpened or retouched. Raster logos are kept at their native pixels (no upscaling). Only cropping, and white (or the logo's own background colour) canvas added where the source had less margin than the even margin of about 8%. `_originals/partner-logos_2026-11-13/` (the rejected recreated set) was not opened or used.

"Name as the logo reads" is what is legible in the artwork itself, not a verified legal name. Every name still needs the client's confirmation before it is typed anywhere as text.
