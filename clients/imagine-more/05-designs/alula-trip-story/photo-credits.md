## Changes made to the photos (CC licences ask that changes be indicated)

- Scaled down and cropped to 9:16.
- The sky was continued upward beyond the top edge of each photo (A: 500 px, B: 262 px, C: 280 px of the 1920 px canvas) using the photo's own top-edge colours, to make room for the headline. In A the ground below the photo fades into the footer colour.
- Overlays in the layout: a blue gradient at the top and a dark brown gradient at the bottom, as in the reference.

## Notes and risks

- Option B is share-alike (CC BY-SA). Whether a promotional story built on the photo must itself be shared under CC BY-SA is a legal question we have not resolved; for a commercial post, A and C (CC BY, no share-alike) are the lower-risk choices. This is not legal advice.
- Subject check: A shows Elephant Rock (Jabal AlFil); B shows a Nabataean tomb façade at Hegra; C shows the mud-brick Old Town below the AlUla cliffs. Each was confirmed by looking at the image and by the Commons title/description (A also by its camera coordinates). No identifiable people appear in any of the three.
- Unsplash (bot check) and Pexels (403) could not be reached from this environment, so no photos were taken from them.
