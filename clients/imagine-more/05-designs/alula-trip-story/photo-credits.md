### Changes made to the photos (v03) — to be indicated with the credit

- Scaled down and cropped to 9:16. B is a crop of the right-hand part of a wide frame: it keeps one balloon; the other two balloons in the original (one carrying a car-brand name) fall outside the crop. Nothing was erased.
- Sky extended upward from the photo's own top-edge colours: A 300 px, B 300 px of the 1920 px canvas; C none. In A the pavement below the photo fades into the footer colour.
- Colour grade (`_work/grade.py`): more contrast, warmer highlights, deeper shadows, gentle vignette. A is graded hardest, C least.
- Layout overlays: gradient at the top (blue on A and B, dark brown on C) and dark brown fade at the bottom.
- Not done: no sky replacement, no added or removed objects, no AI generation, no compositing of different photos.

### Notes and risks (v03)

- **Maraya (option A) — building rights.** Maraya is a modern building. Saudi Arabia has no clear freedom-of-panorama exception, so the photographer's CC BY licence covers the photograph but may not cover commercial use of the building's image; the architect / owner (Royal Commission for AlUla) may hold rights. Using it in an advert is a risk the client must accept or clear. This is not legal advice.
- **Options B and C are share-alike (CC BY-SA 4.0).** Whether a promotional story built on the photo must itself be shared under CC BY-SA is unresolved here. SPA's platform terms, as quoted on Commons, say the content "may be utilized across all media outlets ... and creatively reused". Not legal advice.
- People: option A shows small, distant figures on the forecourt (one walking, back to camera; two at the right edge) and parked vehicles. They are not recognisable at this size but are present.
- Subject check: A is Maraya with the Ashar valley cliffs reflected in its façade (Commons title, and the building is unmistakable). B and C are identified as AlUla by SPA's titles; B's rock field matches AlUla's formations. C's exact location inside AlUla is not stated.
- Sources tried and not used: Unsplash pages (bot check, not bypassed), Commons API (429). 247 Commons candidates were reviewed on contact sheets (`_work/contact_v03_0..5.jpg`).
