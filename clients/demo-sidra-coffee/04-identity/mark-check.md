## Read this first: what this check is and is not

- **This is not a legal trademark clearance.** A formal search and written opinion by a trademark attorney is required before the name or the mark is used, printed or filed for real.
- **No trademark register was searched:** not SAIP (Saudi Arabia), not the GCC trademark system, not the WIPO Global Brand Database, in class 30 (coffee), class 43 (café services), class 35 (retail) or any other class.
- **Results are search-result summaries.** 14 web searches were run on 2026-11-13 in Arabic and English. The titles, links and summaries returned were read. **No linked page was opened.** Every "source" below is a URL that appeared in the results, not a page that was read in full.
- **Logos cannot be compared by text search.** No image search, reverse-image search or logo-similarity search was run. Section 3 only reports whether any text described a similar device.
- **Absence of a hit is weak evidence.** The search tool is US-based, handles Arabic and multi-term queries poorly, and does not index Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, Google Maps or the Saudi commercial register. Small cafés and social-only shops would not appear.
- No domain or social handle availability was checked.

## 1. Same or similar names in coffee, cafés, roasteries and F&B (Saudi Arabia and GCC)

### 1.1 Sidra, Alshaya dining complex: Riyadh and Kuwait

| | |
|---|---|
| What it is | A multi-restaurant dining destination named "Sidra", operated by Alshaya (the Kuwaiti franchise operator). Search summaries describe a first site in Mahboula, Kuwait (announced 2015) and a second in Riyadh (announced April 2018), beside Granada Centre near Exit 9, about 18,000 sq m. The Riyadh launch line-up is reported to include Starbucks, The Cheesecake Factory, P.F. Chang's, Shake Shack, IHOP and others. |
| Where | Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and Mahboula, Kuwait |
| Sources | https://www.alshaya.com/en/media-centre/alshaya-news/introducing-sidra-a-new-dining-destination-in-riyadh/ ; https://www.alshaya.com/en/media-centre/alshaya-news/introducing-sidra-a-new-dining-destination-for-kuwait ; https://www.daleeeel.com/en-kw/article/6243/alshaya-launched-sidra-dining-complex-in-riyadh ; Arabic spelling «مجمع سدره» appears in https://www.bayut.sa/blog/اكتشف-السعودية/مجمع-سدره/ |
| Possibly the same place | A Starbucks store locator lists a branch at "Sidra Mall", Riyadh: https://locations.starbucks.sa/directory/riyadh/sidra-mall . Alshaya operates Starbucks in the region, so this is probably the same complex. **Not confirmed.** |
| Not known | Whether it is still trading under that name in 2026; how the name is spelled in Arabic on site (سدرة or سدره); what its logo looks like; whether Alshaya holds trademark registrations for "Sidra" and in which classes. |

**Rating: High.**
Same word, same city as the client, in food and beverage, owned by a large and well-resourced group. It is a venue name, not a coffee brand, so it is not the same goods. But restaurant and café services (class 43) sit close to a roastery that sells to cafés and may open a counter of its own, and coffee is sold on that site under the name "Starbucks at Sidra".

What it means for a real launch:
- Counsel must check whether "Sidra" / «سدرة» / «سدره» is registered by Alshaya or an affiliate in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait, especially in class 43 and class 35, before anything is filed or printed.
- In Riyadh, "Sidra" spoken aloud in a food context may already mean "the dining complex near Granada". "قهوة في سدرة" could be heard as "coffee at Sidra". This is a real findability and confusion cost even if there is no legal conflict.
- Map and local search for "Sidra coffee Riyadh" will likely return the Starbucks at that site.
- This strengthens the earlier advice in `naming.md` 2.4: always use the full name «سدرة للقهوة» / "Sidra Coffee Roastery", never "Sidra" alone in Riyadh-facing material.

**Rating: not rateable.** This result says nothing reliable about the mark. Logos are rarely described in words, and none of these pages was opened or looked at. The honest status of the seven-bar device is **unknown**, not clear.

Standing risks, unchanged from `licences.md`:
- A row of equal bars is a generic device (barcode, progress bar, calendar strip, equaliser). Similar marks almost certainly exist in other categories. Any distinctiveness comes from the combination: Arabic wordmark, seven bars, one bar in orange-red.
- The wordmark is typeset in an unmodified open-licence font, which is weak as a distinctive element until it is redrawn.

What a real launch needs for the mark: a figurative (device) search by counsel in classes 30, 43 and 35, plus a reverse-image and visual sweep of Saudi and GCC roaster packaging and social accounts by someone who can look at pictures. The Creative Director's visual comparison with three competitor home pages (`01-direction/distinctiveness-check.md`) is the only visual check on file.

## 4. What this means, in order of importance

1. **Do not treat the name as clear.** The Alshaya "Sidra" complex in Riyadh (1.1) is a same-city, same-sector use of the identical word. It needs a legal opinion before any real use. For the system test nothing changes; for a real client this would go to the client as a decision before identity sign-off.
2. **Brief for counsel**, in one list:
   - Identity and similarity search for Sidra / Sedra / Sidrah / سدرة / سدره in classes 30, 43 and 35, Saudi Arabia first, then Kuwait and Qatar, then the rest of the GCC.
   - Specific owners to look for: Alshaya (1.1), the operator of the Qatar Sidra stores (1.3), Sidra Investments LLC (section 2).
   - The variety-name descriptiveness question in class 30 (1.2 and `naming.md` 2.4).
   - A device search for the seven-bar mark.
3. **Usage rule to keep, now with a second reason:** always the full name «سدرة للقهوة» / "Sidra Coffee Roastery". The single word is a variety on a bag and a place on a map.
4. **Checks a person with a phone can still do and this search could not:** Google Maps and delivery apps (HungerStation, Jahez, Talabat) in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam for «سدرة» cafés; Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok handle search; Saudi commercial register name search; domains.
