# Creative direction — amendment 01

> **SYSTEM TEST — FICTIONAL CLIENT.** Invented data for testing Creative Agency OS.

**Amends:** `creative-direction.md` v01 (approved 2026-11-13; that file is unchanged)  **Date:** 2026-11-13  **Prepared by:** Art Director
**Authority:** round-2 rulings in the last row of the decision log in `brand-context.md` (Creative Director acting as client for the test), plus one correction of my own error and one addition from the corporate-designer.
**How to read:** where this amendment and `creative-direction.md` differ, this amendment wins. Everything not mentioned here stands.

## 1. Correction — Ink on Ember contrast (section 2)

`creative-direction.md` gives Ink on Ember as 3.9:1. That figure was calculated on an earlier Ember value and is wrong. **Ink `#1F1B18` on Ember `#C23A20` is 3.19:1** (recalculated 2026-11-13).

- It still clears 3:1 for large text only, with very little margin.
- Rule, tightened: Ink text on Ember is allowed only at 24 px / 18 pt and above, or 19 px / 14 pt Bold and above. Prefer Paper on Ember (4.56:1) for any text. Ink bars on Ember are graphics, not text, and are unaffected.

## 2. The bar device is never a chart (section 4) — ruling (f)

- Steps, stages, days, products or any other content are **never tied to individual bars**: no labels under bars, no numbering of bars, no "step 2 sits under the lit bar".
- Where a layout shows a sequence (for example three steps), it is set on the seven-column grid by the column rules, independent of where the bars fall. If a bar band appears on the same layout, it sits as a separate band, divided from the sequence by clear space or a hairline.

## 3. Logo fallback ladder (section 5) — ruling (g)

Replaces "define the switch sizes in the guidelines" with fixed sizes:

| Size (height on screen) | Version |
|---|---|
| 32 px and above (primary lockup); 48 px and above (bilingual lockup and icon) | Seven bars |
| 16 px up to those thresholds | Small version: one solid rule with the centre seventh lit |
| Below 16 px | Wordmark alone |

Print equivalents are recorded in `brand-context.md` section 5 and are to be confirmed on a proof. The outlined IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold wordmark is accepted for the test only (ruling d); see section 8 below.

## 4. Display headlines and bleeding bars (section 4) — ruling (h)

- **Display headlines may span all seven columns.** The 4 + 3 split in section 4 applies to body and secondary content, not to a display headline, which may run the full width in either language.
- **On social formats, the bar band may bleed off the bottom edge.** The bars are cut by the edge at the bottom only; their tops, their count of seven, the gap rule and the lit fourth bar stay fully visible. Bars do not bleed off the sides or the top, and do not bleed on print or document formats.

## 5. Tagline (section 7) — ruling (a)

- Option 1 is **confirmed**: «تُحمَّص لطلبك، كل أسبوع.» / "Roasted for your order. Every week." Always with its diacritics.
- It remains for wholesale and business material only. **The company profile counts as wholesale material and may carry it.** Retail and home-brewer pieces still do not.

## 6. Contact details (section 7) — rulings (b) and (c)

- **The phone number is removed from every piece** until the client supplies one. No placeholder, no marked gap, no empty row.
- **The only enquiry channel is the email `hello@sidra-coffee.example`.** No social handle, direct-message channel, website address or messaging-app number may be shown or implied.

## 7. What no piece may imply — ruling (i), restated for designers

Delivery timing and a roast date on the bag remain unconfirmed. No layout may show a date stamp, a day name, a countdown, a calendar, or a "delivered by" line. This is why section 2 above matters: a labelled bar would read as a day.

## 8. Wordmark redraw is now a distinctiveness requirement

`distinctiveness-check.md` found that Camel Step's website is set in IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Section 5 of `creative-direction.md` already asks for a custom-drawn wordmark, "not simply typed". The outlined-type wordmark accepted for the test must be redrawn before any real use, so that the mark is not built from the same letterforms a category leader uses for its text.

## 9. A4 document type ladder (addition to section 3)

The four-step scale (0.65 / 1 / 1.6 / 5) stays the identity scale. On A4 documents with the Latin block held to three columns, the display step does not fit, so two steps are added **for A4 documents only**, as introduced by the corporate-designer in `06-profiles/content-outline.md`:

| Level | Scale | Latin | Arabic | Line height EN / AR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Caption | 0.65 | 6.5 pt SemiBold, uppercase, +12% | 7.5 pt Regular | 1.4 / 1.6 |
| Body | 1 | 10 pt Regular | 11.5 pt Regular | 1.45 / 1.75 |
| Sub-head | 1.6 | 16 pt Medium | 18.5 pt Medium | 1.3 / 1.5 |
| **Section title (added)** | 2.8 | **28 pt** Bold, -1.5% | **32 pt** Bold | 1.1 / 1.3 |
| **Cover title (added)** | 4 | **40 pt** Bold, -1.5% | **46 pt** Bold | 1.05 / 1.3 |

- Tracking applies to the Latin only; Arabic is never tracked.
- One section title per page; the cover title appears on the cover only.
- These two steps are not used on social, presentation or packaging formats, which keep the four-step scale.
- The identity-designer records this ladder in `04-identity/03-system/system.md` and `tokens.json`.
