## 1. The idea

The week has seven beats and one of them is your roast. The identity is a seven-part system with one part lit in Ember. It makes the approved promise, «طلبك يُحمَّص هذا الأسبوع.» / "Your order, roasted this week.", visible before any word is read. Arabic leads every piece; English is the equal second.

## 2. Colour

| Name | HEX | Role | Share of a typical layout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper — ورق | `#F3ECE0` | Default background | about 45% |
| Crema — كريما | `#E3C9A0` | Warm second ground: panels, alternate pages, inside of folders, product stages, the 250 g bag | about 25% |
| Ink — حبر | `#1F1B18` | All text; the six unlit bars; rules | about 15% |
| Ember — جمر | `#C23A20` | The lit bar; one headline or one solid field per layout; the 1 kg bag | about 10% |
| Ash — رماد | `#6F6861` | Captions and small technical text, on Paper only | about 5% |

- Warmth comes from Crema. Every piece of more than one surface uses Crema on at least one of them. A layout that is only Paper, Ink and Ember is too cold and is not on brand.
- Ember is used once per layout as a field or a headline, plus the lit bar. It is never a body-text colour.
- Allowed text pairs (contrast calculated): Ink on Paper 14.6:1; Ink on Crema 10.7:1; Paper on Ink 14.6:1; Paper on Ember 4.6:1; Ember on Paper 4.6:1 at 24 px and above only; Ash on Paper 4.7:1.
- Not allowed for text: Ink on Ember below 24 px (3.9:1); Ash on Crema; Ember on Crema; Crema on Paper.
- No green, no gradients, no tints. CMYK and Pantone values are for the identity-designer to set in `03-system/`.

## 3. Typography

**Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Latin: IBM Plex Sans.** Both SIL OFL, from `assets/fonts/`. The Latin file in the repo is a variable font: generate or obtain static instances (Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold) for print so the fonts embed by name. Record both in `04-identity/licences.md`.

| Level | Arabic | Latin | Line height (AR / EN) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display headline | Bold 700 | Bold 700, tracking -1.5% | 1.3 / 1.05 |
| Sub-head | Medium 500 | Medium 500 | 1.5 / 1.3 |
| Body | Regular 400 | Regular 400 | 1.7–1.8 / 1.45 |
| Label on product (name, weight) | SemiBold 600 / Bold 700 | SemiBold 600 | 1.5 / 1.3 |
| Caption | Regular 400, Ash | SemiBold 600, uppercase, tracking +12%, Ash | 1.6 / 1.4 |
| Large figures (250 g, 1 kg) | Bold 700 with the unit in Arabic (1 كغ) | Light 300, width 85% | — |

- Scale: body 1, sub-head 1.6, display 5 (for example 21 / 34 / 104 px on the board). Use only these steps plus caption at 0.65.
- Arabic is set 15–20% larger than the Latin beside it so the two read as equal.
- Arabic never has tracking, uppercase styling, faux bold or slant. Tracking applies to Latin captions and the Latin logo line only.
- Numerals are Western (0123) in both languages. Arabic punctuation in Arabic text: ، ؛ ؟ «».
- In bilingual layouts, Arabic sits right and Latin left, each in its own block. Never line by line.

## 4. The seven-bar system

**The bar row**
- Always exactly seven bars, equal in size, in one row. Six in Ink (Paper when on Ink or Ember), one lit.
- **The lit bar is always the fourth, the centre bar.** This is fixed and illustrative. It does not stand for a roast day, because the roast day is not confirmed. Never move it, never label bars with day names, never light two.
- Lit bar colour: Ember on Paper, Crema and Ink; Paper on Ember.
- Gap between bars = 1/5 of a bar's width, at every size.
- Two proportions only: **band** (each bar taller than wide, between 1:2 and 1:4) for packaging and large fields; **rule** (each bar 2 wide to 1 high) for the logo and as a divider.
- The row is never rotated, curved, outlined, animated out of order or used as a chart.
- Pattern: band rows stacked with the same gap, lit bars aligned in one column. Three rows minimum.

**The layout grid**
- Every layout is seven equal columns between the margins, with no gutter for full-bleed bands and a gutter of 1/5 column for text layouts.
- Arabic layouts: text spans 4 columns from the right, secondary content 3 from the left. English-only layouts mirror this.
- Section breaks are a 2 px Ink hairline at full width. Corners are square. No shadows on flat graphics.

## 5. Logo brief (for the identity-designer)

- **Primary mark:** the Arabic word سدرة drawn as a custom wordmark, sturdy and low in contrast, matched to IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold but not simply typed in it. Beneath it, the bar row in rule proportion, exactly as wide as the wordmark. Beneath that, "SIDRA COFFEE" in IBM Plex Sans SemiBold capitals, tracked to the same width.
- **Lockups:** Arabic with bars (primary); bilingual as above; Latin-led ("SIDRA COFFEE" over the bars) for English-only contexts; icon-only (the seven bars in band proportion inside a square). Full colour, Ink only, and reversed (Paper) versions of each.
- **The two dots of the ة stay as ordinary dots.** They are not coloured or turned into a symbol.
- **Size test, required:** every lockup at 16 px high on screen and 10 mm wide in print. The seven bars must still count as seven and the lit bar must still be seen as different.
- **Barcode fallback, in this order:** (1) if the bars read as a barcode, widen them towards the 2:1 rule proportion and keep the gap rule; (2) below the size at which seven bars hold, replace the row with one solid Ink rule whose centre seventh is Ember; (3) below that, the wordmark alone. Define the switch sizes in the guidelines. Never place the bar row next to a real barcode on packaging; keep them on different faces or at least one bar-row width apart.
- Define clear space as the height of the letter د in the wordmark. Check both the mark and the name for obvious conflicts and state plainly that this is not a legal trademark clearance.

## 6. Graphic language

- Flat colour, hairlines, the bar row and type. No illustration, no ornament, no icons of beans, cups, trees, camels or flames.
- Facts at headline size: one short factual line per layout, numbers before adjectives. Weights and formats are set large.
- **No imagery of people**, including hands. If photography is used: beans, bags and equipment only, shot flat and frontal on Paper or Crema, in even warm light, without props.
- Packaging: 1 kg (wholesale) bag in Ember with Paper type and an Ink band; 250 g bag in Crema or Paper with Ink type and an Ember lit bar.

## 7. Copy rules that affect design

- **Tagline:** option 1, always with its diacritics: «تُحمَّص لطلبك، كل أسبوع.» / "Roasted for your order. Every week." **Wholesale material only.** It does not appear on retail or home-brewer pieces; those use the name and the plain facts. The board shows it on the 250 g bag: do not follow the board on this point.
- In English running copy, pair the name with "roastery" (for example "Sidra Coffee roastery"), not "Sidra" alone.
- Never place "Sidra" or «سدرة» beside an origin or variety word on any label or layout. Keep the brand name and the coffee descriptor in separate blocks with a rule or clear space between them.
- No roast day, delivery day, awards, client names or the placeholder phone number anywhere.

## 8. Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Lead with Arabic; give English its own block | Translate line by line or put Latin first on bilingual pieces |
| Use Crema on at least one surface of every piece | Build layouts from Paper, Ink and Ember alone |
| Keep seven bars, the fourth lit, the same everywhere | Move the lit bar, add day names, or change the count |
| Use Ember once per layout | Set body text in Ember or flood every surface with it |
| Set one fact large | Use superlatives or decorative copy |
| Keep the layout on seven columns | Round corners, add gradients, shadows or textures |
| Keep the palette as specified | Introduce green, or black and kraft as a lead look |
| Show product and equipment only | Show people, hands, or heritage motifs |
