## 3. Strategy (approved: 2026-11-13, test approval by Creative Director — Direction A "The weekly roast")


- **Purpose / vision / mission:** Purpose: so that an independent café never has to wonder what is in the bag or when it will arrive. Vision: to be the roaster Riyadh's independent cafés name first. Mission: roast to order every week for Riyadh's independent cafés, sell our coffee to home brewers, and help café teams brew it well.
- **Values:** On schedule (على الموعد); Plain facts (كلام واضح); On the café's side (في صفّ المقهى); Hospitality (ضيافة).
- **Target audience:** Primary: independent café owners in Riyadh. Secondary: home brewers aged 22 to 40 buying online, Arabic first. Motives are assumptions pending validation.
- **Competitors:** Camel Step, Elixir Bunn, Brew92 (home pages viewed 2026-11-13; see `04-identity/01-direction/distinctiveness-check_v02.md` and `02-research/competitor-audit_v02.md`).
- **Positioning statement:** For independent café owners in Riyadh, and for home brewers who want the same standard, Sidra Coffee is the Riyadh roastery built around a weekly roast-to-order rhythm rather than around its own café counters: a supplier that roasts each café's order in that week's roast. (Clause "and runs no cafés of its own": TBC, client to confirm.)
- **Personality (is / is not):** Dependable, not rigid or corporate. Rooted and Arabic-led, not folkloric. Plain-spoken, not technical for show or boastful. Hospitable, not chatty or salesy.
- **Brand promise and proof points:** Promise (wholesale-facing material only, ruling aa): "Your order, roasted this week." / «طلبك يُحمَّص هذا الأسبوع.» Proof (verified): roasts to order weekly; based in Riyadh; weekly roasted-to-order wholesale; 250 g and 1 kg; barista courses; monthly subscription; founded 2023. TBC: roast date on bag, published roast and delivery days, no own cafés, roast-to-order for single online orders.
- **Tagline (EN / AR):** CONFIRMED 2026-11-13 (test): "Roasted for your order. Every week." / «تُحمَّص لطلبك، كل أسبوع.»

## 4. Voice


- **Tone in Arabic:** clear modern Arabic (فصحى مبسّطة) with light Gulf warmth [register is an assumption from research, to confirm]; direct, specific, peer to peer; written natively, not translated. Common coffee terms are written as people say them: V60 stays in Latin script; espresso is written إسبريسو in Arabic text.
- **Tone in English:** plain, specific, short sentences; confident without superlatives; same facts as the Arabic, not a literal rendering of it.
- **Words we use / avoid:** Use: محمصة، يُحمَّص لطلبك، كل أسبوع، طازجة / roastery, roasted to order, every week, fresh. ("On schedule / على الموعد" stays a value but is not used in public copy until roast and delivery days can be published.) Avoid: الأفضل، الأجود، رقم 1، فاخر، حصري، شغف، رحلة / best, finest, premium, award-winning, passion, journey; any claim about awards, customers, certifications or Saudi-grown beans.
- **Numerals:** Western (0123)

## 5. Visual identity (v03 accepted for system test only, 2026-11-13 — not client-approved)

- **Creative direction (one sentence):** Weekly Beat — a seven-part system with the centre part lit in Ember; see `04-identity/01-direction/creative-direction.md`
- **Logo files and usage:** `04-identity/02-logo/` (outlined SVG). Primary = Arabic wordmark سدرة over the seven-bar rule row; bilingual adds SIDRA COFFEE; Latin-led for English-only; icon = three stacked rows of seven bars in a square, centre column lit (v02). Clear space = height of the letter د (0.27 × logo width). Seven bars from 32 px high / 10 mm wide (bilingual 48 px / 18 mm); below that the `-small` version (solid rule, centre seventh lit) down to 16 px / 6 mm; below that the wordmark alone. Print minimums to be confirmed on a proof.
- **Colours:**

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper — ورق | #F3ECE0 | 243 236 224 | 4 6 12 0 (approx.) | waived for system test; required before print | Default background, about 45% |
| Crema — كريما | #E3C9A0 | 227 201 160 | 10 20 40 0 (approx.) | waived for system test; required before print | Warm second ground, about 25% |
| Ink — حبر | #1F1B18 | 31 27 24 | 60 60 60 85 (approx.); text 0 0 0 100 | waived for system test; required before print | Text, unlit bars, rules, about 15% |
| Ember — جمر | #C23A20 | 194 58 32 | 15 90 100 5 (approx.) | waived for system test; required before print | Lit bar; one field or headline per layout; 1 kg bag, about 10% |
| Ash — رماد | #6F6861 | 111 104 97 | 50 47 50 20 (approx.) | waived for system test; required before print | Captions on Paper only, about 5% |

- **Typefaces:** Arabic — IBM Plex Sans Arabic; Latin — IBM Plex Sans (both SIL OFL 1.1); Arabic set 15–20% larger than adjacent Latin; scale 0.65 / 1 / 1.6 / 5; licences in `04-identity/licences.md`
- **Imagery and graphic style:** flat colour, 2 px Ink hairlines, the seven-bar row (rule 2:1, band 1:2 to 1:4, gap 1/5 bar, fourth bar lit), seven-column grid. No icons or illustration. Photography of beans, bags and equipment only, flat and frontal on Paper or Crema.
- **Do not:** move the lit bar, light two, change the count or label bars with days; use green, gradients, tints, shadows or rounded corners; set body text in Ember; put Ash or Ember text on Crema; track Arabic; show people or hands; place "Sidra" / «سدرة» beside an origin or variety word; place the bar row beside a barcode.

- **Additions after amendments 01 and 02 (2026-11-13):** the "one lit bar, always seven" rule counts per row, so the stacked pattern and icon v02 (three rows of seven, centre column lit) are exempt. On web pages Ember may be used for the one primary action button per screenful in place of an Ember field or headline; a persistent header button is Ink. Ink on Ember is 3.19:1 (large text and shapes only). Motion: bars build in reading order within 1.0 s, any hold shows seven bars or none, the wordmark appears whole. Current files: strategy summary v03 (`03-strategy/`), guidelines v04, tokens and system v03, logo set in `04-identity/02-logo/` (SVG, `png/`, `pdf/`).

- **Name rule (mitigation for the recorded name risk):** always the full name — «سدرة للقهوة» in Arabic and "Sidra Coffee" (with "roastery" in running copy) in English. Never «سدرة» or "Sidra" alone in text, and never beside an origin or variety word. The wordmark «سدرة» inside the logo is the only exception.

- **Never** pun on or refer to «سدرة المنتهى» in any copy (from `03-strategy/naming.md`).
