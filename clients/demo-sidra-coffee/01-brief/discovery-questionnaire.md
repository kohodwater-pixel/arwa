# Discovery questionnaire

Ask only what the supplied material does not already answer.

## Business
1. What does the company do, in one sentence a customer would use?
2. When and why was it founded?
3. What are the main products or services, and which earns the most?
4. Where do you operate now, and where in three years?
5. What are the business goals for the next 12 months?

## Audience
6. Who are your best customers? Describe one real example.
7. What problem do they come to you to solve?
8. Why do they choose you? Why do some choose someone else?

## Competition
9. Who are your three main competitors?
10. What do you do that they cannot or will not?

## Brand
11. Three words that should describe the brand; three it must never be.
12. If the brand were a person, how would they speak and dress?
13. Brands you admire (any sector), and why?
14. What must stay from the current identity? What must go?
15. Any colours, symbols or words to avoid for cultural, religious or sector reasons?

## Project
16. What exactly do you need delivered?
17. Which languages, and which is primary?
18. Where will the brand appear first (signage, packaging, app, social, tenders)?
19. Deadline and any fixed dates (launch, exhibition, tender)?
20. Who gives feedback and who has final approval?
