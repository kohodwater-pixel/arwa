## The bar row

Every slide carries the seven-bar device at its foot: the tall band on the cover, the thin row on slides 2 to 5. On slides 2, 3 and 4 it comes from the layout, in the same position, so slides the client adds from those layouts carry it too. It is a device only (ruling f): no step, panel, bullet or number is tied to a bar, and the content blocks do not sit on the seven columns in a way that pairs them with individual bars.

## Name rule

Per `brand-context.md` section 5, the name appears in text only in full: «سدرة للقهوة» on slides 1 and 2. The wordmark inside the logo is the only place «سدرة» stands alone. No slide places the name beside an origin or variety word.
