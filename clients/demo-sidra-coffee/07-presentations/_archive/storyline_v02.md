## Changes from v01

- Slide 2: the two questions about roast date and arrival, and the line promising to answer them, are removed. Title no longer mentions when an order arrives.
- Slide 3: steps moved off the bars onto their own evenly spaced three-column row with one-line bodies of similar length; the title is now a how-it-works statement so it does not repeat slide 2. The Ember number is gone; Ember appears only in the lit bar.
- Slide 4: caption rewritten; the list is now a real bulleted placeholder; "ليخرج الكوب كما يجب" dropped from the training line to keep each bullet on one line.
- Slide 5: reduced to «راسلنا» plus the email. The first-order line and the city line are removed.
- File: brand master with theme fonts and colours and four layouts; Latin runs in IBM Plex Sans; `sldSz` untyped at 12192000 × 6858000 EMU; slide counter is a real slide-number field at 18 pt; `FONTS.md` added.
