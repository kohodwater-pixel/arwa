# UX brief — Sidra Coffee wholesale landing page

> **SYSTEM TEST — FICTIONAL CLIENT.** Invented data for testing Creative Agency OS.

**Version:** v01  **Date:** 2026-11-13  **Prepared by:** UI/UX Designer  **Language:** Arabic (RTL). English version not in this scope.

## One conversion goal

An independent café owner in Riyadh submits the form to **request a wholesale sample or a contact about supply**. Everything on the page leads to that form; there is no second goal (no shop, no subscription sign-up).

## Users and what they want

| User | Wants to know | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Independent café owner, Riyadh (primary) | Is this a dependable local roaster? How does ordering work? What can I buy? How do I start? | Brief section 5; motives are assumptions (brand-context) |
| Home brewer (secondary, not targeted here) | Sees the subscription in one line only | Brief section 5 |

## Key tasks

1. Understand the offer in one screen: roasted to order, every week, from Riyadh.
2. See how the weekly roast works (three steps, the seven-bar device).
3. Send a sample or contact request, or copy the email address.

## Section order

Header (logo, CTA) → Hero (promise, value proposition, CTA) → How the weekly roast works → What we offer → Contact form → Footer.

There is **no proof section** (testimonials, client logos, counts): the brief lists these as not available. Verified facts stand in: Riyadh, founded 2023, weekly roast to order, 250 g and 1 kg.

## Content inventory (all from the brief and `03-strategy/messaging.md`)

- Tagline option 1 (approved for wholesale material), value proposition, café-owner key messages marked [V].
- Four products and services from brief section 4.
- Contact: hello@sidra-coffee.example; Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The phone number is a placeholder and is not shown.
- Left out on purpose: roast day, delivery day, minimum order, prices, "no cafés of our own", origins, any customer or award claim.

## Devices and accessibility

Mobile first (390 px), desktop at 1440 px. WCAG AA: contrast, visible focus, 44 px targets, text in rem. Western numerals.

## Success measures (to be set up at build; no baseline exists)

- Form completion rate (submissions / visitors who reach the form).
- Hero CTA click-through to the form.
- Share of submissions choosing "sample" against "contact".

## Assumptions to confirm

- That Sidra offers samples to cafés at all, and on what terms. The page says "request a sample" and nothing about price or quantity.
- That replies go out by email.
