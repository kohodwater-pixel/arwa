## Notes

- The logo artwork is the approved `04-identity/02-logo/demo-sidra-coffee_logo_primary-colour_ar_v01.svg`; its geometry is copied unchanged into the animation.
- Clear space: the logo is 540 px wide, so the required clear space is 146 px; the nearest edge is 270 px away.
- Reading time: one word, visible in full for 1.0 s.
- **The first frame is empty Paper**, which follows from "the bars draw in". It does not work as a video thumbnail; use the last frame as the cover image.
- The fade in scene 6 passes through intermediate shades of Ink for half a second. The direction says "no tints" for static layouts; whether that applies to a transition is for the art-director to say. A wipe was tried first and rejected: half-way through it hid the two dots of the ة, so the word read as «سدره».
