# Brand strategy — Sidra Coffee (سدرة للقهوة)

## 1. Company study (what we actually know)

Sidra Coffee is a specialty coffee roastery founded in 2023 and based in Riyadh [V]. It roasts to order weekly [V] and sells roasted beans in 250 g and 1 kg, single-origin and house blends; weekly roasted-to-order wholesale supply for cafés; a monthly home subscription; and short barista courses for café staff [V]. Business goal: win the first 20 café accounts and look credible to wholesale buyers [V, brief]. No client list, customer numbers, awards, certifications, team names or sales figures exist for use, and none are implied anywhere in this document.

**Evidence caveats.** Audience motives are assumptions (no interviews) [R]. Competitor messaging and visuals are unverified recall; only names, cities, founding years (two of three) and Elixir Bunn's branch network are sourced [R]. This strategy therefore positions against the *structure* of the category, not against anyone's look or tagline.

## 2. Purpose, vision, mission, values [P]

- **Purpose:** so that an independent café never has to wonder what is in the bag or when it will arrive.
- **Vision:** to be the roaster Riyadh's independent cafés name first when asked who supplies them. (Ambition, not a claim.)
- **Mission:** roast every order in the weekly roast, for Riyadh's independent cafés and for people who brew at home, and help café teams brew it well.
- **Values:**
  1. **On schedule — على الموعد.** The weekly roast is a commitment, not a marketing line.
  2. **Plain facts — كلام واضح.** We say what it is, when it was roasted and what to do with it. No superlatives.
  3. **On the café's side — في صفّ المقهى.** The café's cup is the product; our name comes second.
  4. **Hospitality — ضيافة.** Expertise is shared generously, never used to talk down.

## 3. Audience

| | Café owner (primary; drives the 20-account goal) | Home brewer (secondary) |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Independent café owners in Riyadh [V]; owner-operators with 1 to 3 branches [A] | Aged 22 to 40, buy specialty beans online, Arabic first, comfortable in English [V] |
| Wants | "A reliable local roaster" [V, brief wording]; the same cup every week [A] | Fresh beans with a clear date, the right coffee for their brewer [A] |
| Fears | Late or inconsistent supply; a supplier whose own cafés compete nearby [A / R inference] | Stale beans, paying for hype, being talked down to [A] |
| Persuaded by | Specifics: schedule, sizes, a person who answers [A] | Clarity and warmth, coffee terms used naturally [A] |

Personas are deliberately not named or given invented biographies: the evidence does not support them. Recommended validation: five short conversations with Riyadh café owners [R].

## 4. Competitive frame

Category as the buyer sees it: "which local roaster supplies my café?" not "which coffee brand do I like?". Audited: Camel Step (Riyadh, 2013; the pioneer, heritage) [R, sourced], Elixir Bunn (Riyadh, 2014; connoisseur sourcing; own branches in several Saudi cities and London) [R, sourced], Brew92 (Jeddah-born, known for consistency in Riyadh) [R, sourced]. Sourcing detail and competition entries are the category's standard proof [R]; Sidra has neither on record and should not imitate that language. Open territory: the roaster that is a supplier first, Arabic-led, and whose proof is a schedule [R inference].

## 5. Strategic direction

### Recommended — Direction A: "The weekly roast" (محمصة الموعد)

**Idea:** Sidra is the roastery organised around one dependable rhythm: your order, roasted this week. Reliability is the brand; origin stories are supporting detail.

**Why:** it is built on the only verified differentiating fact (roasts to order weekly), it answers the brief's own phrase ("reliable local roaster"), it serves both audiences with one promise (reliability for the café, freshness for the home), and it needs no awards or client names to be credible.

**Positioning statement:**
> For independent café owners in Riyadh, and for home brewers who want the same standard, Sidra Coffee is the Riyadh roastery built around a weekly roast-to-order rhythm rather than around its own café counters: an Arabic-first supplier that roasts what you ordered in that week's roast **[and runs no cafés of its own — include only if the client confirms]**.

**Arabic (written natively, not a translation):**
> سدرة محمصة من الرياض، تعمل على إيقاع واحد: طلبك يُحمَّص هذا الأسبوع. نورّد للمقاهي المستقلة ولمن يحضّر قهوته في بيته، ونتكلم العربية أولًا.

**Competitor test:** Camel Step would lead with being first and with heritage; Elixir Bunn with sourcing, and it runs its own branches [R, sourced]; Brew92 is Jeddah-born and English-named [R]. None could use the statement unchanged. **Honest limit:** the strongest clause is "runs no cafés of its own". Without client confirmation the statement rests on weekly roast-to-order plus Arabic-first, which competitors could match operationally; whether they already roast to order weekly was not checked.

### Alternative — Direction B: "Under the sidr" (في ظل السدرة)

**Idea:** an Arabic-first hospitality brand for the home brewer; the sidr tree as a place of shade and welcome [A: cultural meaning from general knowledge, not sourced]. Warmer and more distinctive on social and packaging, but it speaks mainly to the secondary audience, gives a wholesale buyer no reason to switch, and leans on symbolism rather than a verified fact. **Not recommended as the lead;** its hospitality note is kept inside Direction A as a personality trait.

## 6. Personality (Direction A)

| Trait | Is | Is not | What a designer or writer does with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dependable — يُعتمد عليها** | Regular, exact, the same every week | Rigid, corporate, cold | A consistent, repeatable system; dates and weights given real prominence; nothing that changes for novelty's sake |
| **Rooted — بنت الرياض** | Local, Arabic-led, at ease in its own language | Folkloric, nostalgic, camel-and-desert heritage | Arabic leads every lockup and headline, English as the equal second; local reference through restraint, not ornament |
| **Plain-spoken — واضحة** | Specific, short, factual | Technical for show, poetic, boastful | Short headlines, numbers before adjectives, generous space, legible small text |
| **Hospitable — مِضيافة** | Warm, generous with knowledge | Chatty, cute, salesy | Warmth carried by colour, materials and wording, never by pictures of people (brief constraint) |

Standing constraints for the visual stage [V, brief]: no imagery of people; no green-and-white combinations close to large international coffee chains (the tree name pulls towards green; resist it).

## 7. Promise and proof points

**Promise:** *Your order, roasted this week.* — «طلبك يُحمَّص هذا الأسبوع.»

Proof points available today [V]:
1. Roasts to order weekly.
2. Based in Riyadh; a local supplier to Riyadh cafés.
3. Weekly roasted-to-order wholesale supply, with 1 kg and 250 g formats.
4. Short barista courses for café staff.
5. Monthly subscription for home delivery.
6. Founded 2023 (state plainly; do not dress it up as heritage).

Proof points that would make the promise visible, **each needing client confirmation before use** [A]: roast date printed on every bag; a named weekly roast day and delivery day; no cafés of its own, now or planned; weekly roast-to-order also applying to single online orders (the brief states it generally and for wholesale; the subscription is monthly).

## 8. Open questions for the client

1. Does Sidra run, or plan to run, its own cafés? (Decides whether "partner, not rival" can be said.)
2. Can a roast day and delivery day be published, and is the roast date printed on the bag?
3. Does roast-to-order apply to individual online orders?
4. Is the name's link to the sidr tree intended, and may it be referred to?

### 4. Voice

- **Tone in Arabic:** clear modern Arabic (فصحى مبسّطة) with light Gulf warmth [register is an assumption from research, to confirm]; direct, specific, peer to peer; written natively, not translated. Common coffee terms stay in Latin script where people use them (V60, espresso).

- **Words we use / avoid:** Use: محمصة، يُحمَّص لطلبك، كل أسبوع، على الموعد، طازجة / roastery, roasted to order, every week, on schedule, fresh.
