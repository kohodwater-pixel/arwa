# Quality standards

A deliverable is done when it passes every point below. "Almost" is a fail.

## Strategic fit

- It answers the brief and can be traced to the approved strategy.
- It is right for the audience in `brand-context.md`, not for the designer's taste.

## Brand consistency

- Only approved logos, colours and typefaces are used, with correct clear space and minimum sizes.
- Tone of voice matches the brand voice in both languages.

## Craft

- A clear hierarchy: the eye knows where to go first, second and third.
- Everything sits on a grid; spacing is consistent and deliberate.
- No widows, orphans, rivers or awkward line breaks; no stretched or pixelated images.
- Text contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text) on screen.
- Arabic follows `arabic-design.md` without exception.

## Accuracy

- Zero spelling or grammar errors in any language. Names, numbers, phone numbers, URLs and legal lines are checked against the source.
- No placeholder text, no lorem ipsum, no sample images in anything shown to a client.
- Every factual claim about the client or market has a source or is marked as an assumption.

## Technical

- Files follow `file-delivery.md` for naming, format, colour mode, resolution and bleed.
- Files open cleanly on another machine: fonts embedded or packaged, links collected.

## Review

- Every project passes the mandatory ten-point review in `quality-control/project-review.md`. Review notes are shown, not hidden, and work is not approved until every critical and major issue is fixed and re-reviewed.

- The maker checks their own work against the relevant checklist in `quality-control/` before handing it over.
- The quality-reviewer then reviews independently and reports each failed item with its location and the fix required. Failed work goes back; it is not patched by the reviewer silently.
