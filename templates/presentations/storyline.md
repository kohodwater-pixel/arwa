## Pitch / credentials deck skeleton

| # | Slide | Title states… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Who and what |
| 2 | The situation | What is true for the audience today |
| 3 | The problem / opportunity | Why that cannot continue |
| 4 | Our answer | The idea in one sentence |
| 5–7 | How it works | Three supporting points, one per slide |
| 8 | Proof | Results, case study, numbers |
| 9 | Who we are | Why us; team or credentials |
| 10 | Plan | Scope, timeline, phases |
| 11 | Investment | Cost or the ask |
| 12 | Next step | Exactly what happens next and when |
| 13 | Contact | Names and details |

## Master layouts to build

Cover · Section divider · Title + text · Title + two columns · Title + image · Full-bleed image · Big number / statistic · Chart · Table · Quote · Team · Timeline · Closing

## Rules

- Slide titles are full statements; reading only the titles tells the whole story
- One idea per slide
- Live decks: under about 30 words per slide. Read-only decks may carry more, in a clear hierarchy
