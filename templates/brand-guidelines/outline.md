# Brand guidelines outline

| # | Section | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction | What this document is and how to use it |
| 2 | Brand foundation | Purpose, vision, mission, values, positioning, personality |
| 3 | Voice | Tone in Arabic and English, do / don't examples, tagline |
| 4 | Logo | Concept and meaning; primary, secondary, icon; Arabic, Latin, bilingual lockups |
| 5 | Logo usage | Clear space, minimum size, colour versions, backgrounds, placement |
| 6 | Logo misuse | 8–10 examples of what not to do |
| 7 | Colour | Primary, secondary, neutrals with HEX / RGB / CMYK / Pantone; proportions; accessible pairs |
| 8 | Typography | Arabic and Latin families, weights, hierarchy, sizes, line height, alignment; fallback system fonts |
| 9 | Graphic elements | Patterns, shapes, lines, how they are built and used |
| 10 | Iconography | Style, stroke, grid, examples |
| 11 | Imagery | Photography and illustration style, do / don't, treatment |
| 12 | Layout | Grid, margins, RTL and LTR rules, bilingual layout |
| 13 | Applications | Stationery, social media, presentation, signage, packaging, digital, vehicles, uniforms as relevant |
| 14 | Files and contact | What is in the delivery package, font licences, who to ask |

Levels: **essential** (sections 4–8, about 12 pages), **standard** (all sections, 30–40 pages), **comprehensive** (with detailed application specs, 60+ pages). Agree the level in the brief.
