# Company profile outline

Cut any section without real content. Typical length: 16–32 pages.

| # | Section | Content | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Name, logo, tagline, year | 1 |
| 2 | Contents | Only if 20+ pages | 1 |
| 3 | Chairman / CEO message | Short, personal, signed | 1 |
| 4 | About us | Who we are, since when, where; 3–4 key figures | 1–2 |
| 5 | Vision, mission, values | One line each; values with one-line meaning | 1–2 |
| 6 | Services / products | Grouped; what it is, who it is for, the benefit | 2–6 |
| 7 | Sectors served | Icons or imagery per sector | 1 |
| 8 | Why us | 4–6 differentiators with proof | 1 |
| 9 | Projects / case studies | Client, challenge, what we did, result, photo | 2–8 |
| 10 | Facts and figures | Infographic: years, projects, staff, coverage | 1 |
| 11 | Clients and partners | Logos (with permission) | 1–2 |
| 12 | Team / structure | Leadership photos and titles, or org chart | 1–2 |
| 13 | Certifications and memberships | ISO, licences, classifications, awards | 1 |
| 14 | HSE / quality / CSR | If relevant to the sector | 1 |
| 15 | Contact | Addresses, phone, email, web, social, map, QR | 1 |
| 16 | Back cover | Logo and contact line | 1 |

## Bilingual structure — choose one

- **Two editions:** separate Arabic and English files (cleanest)
- **Flip book:** Arabic from the right cover, English from the left, meeting in the middle
- **Mirrored spreads:** Arabic on the right page, English on the left, shared imagery

## Specs to confirm

Size (A4 portrait / A4 landscape / square 210 mm), binding, paper, print run, and whether an interactive screen PDF is needed.
